## English

**Privacy policy for Cédric Chai's comment responder**

*Last updated: November 24, 2026*

**Who.** This tool is mine, Cédric Chai. I am its only user, for my own Instagram accounts, @cedricchai_ia and @cedricchai_ai. Nobody else can connect to it.

**What it is for.** When you comment on one of my posts with the requested word (for example "QUOTE"), you are asking me for a free resource. The tool helps me send it to you: one private message with the link, and one reply under your comment. Once per person.

**What it reads.** Only public comments written under my own posts: your username, the technical ID of your account and of your comment, the comment text, its date, and the replies under it. It does not read your profile, your private messages or your posts.

**What it keeps.** On my own computers, and nowhere else:
- a log: your username, the technical IDs, the date, the type of message sent and its result. It is there so I never write to you twice;
- the reply batches I review before sending: your username, the date and the beginning of your comment (200 characters at most).

**Who has access.** Me, and the AI assistants that help me prepare and check the replies (Claude, by Anthropic). Instagram receives the reply I send you, since it goes through Instagram. I sell nothing, I share nothing with anyone else, and I do no targeted advertising and keep no contact list.

**How long.** Twelve months at most after my reply. Then it is deleted.

**Your rights.** You can ask what I hold about you, have it corrected or have it deleted. You can also contact your data protection authority.

**How to have your data deleted.** Write to me at cedricchai.contact@gmail.com, or by private message on Instagram, with your username. I delete your lines from the log and the batches within thirty days and confirm it to you. You can also delete your comment: the tool will no longer read it.

**Terms of use.** There is no account to create and nothing to pay. By commenting with the requested word, you agree to receive one private message and one public reply from me. The code and guide I send are free, provided as is, with no warranty. I may stop this service at any time.

**Contact.** cedricchai.contact@gmail.com
